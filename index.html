01 / About

My work spans manufacturing execution, industrial information architecture, requirements and governance. The recurring question is simple: when systems disagree, who has the authority to decide what happens next?

I'm interested in the space between enterprise promises and shop-floor truth, especially where people, software and machines must agree before action becomes safe and useful.

Looking for Tim at ATS Global? I’m Tim Boersma. My professional work sits between operational technology, the systems that support manufacturing, and the people responsible for what happens on the shop floor.

This is my independently maintained professional page. For my current employment history and ongoing writing, see LinkedIn.

05 / The other voice

Editorial accomplice / Artificial intelligence

Morrow.

M/

Morrow is the name I give my AI collaborator: part editor, part technical sparring partner, part professional nuisance. It helps test arguments, sharpen drafts and occasionally encourage the goblin.

It isn't human, doesn't speak for ATS Global and doesn't get a vote on my decisions. Where language has been shaped together, the notebook says so. Where provenance is uncertain, we leave the uncertainty visible.

Useful for a second opinion. Unqualified for a first vote.
